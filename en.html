Kim Joo-in Internal Medicine Clinic (김주인내과) is an English-speaking internal medicine and pulmonology clinic, a 5-minute walk from Seomyeon Station Exit 7 in Busan. Dr. Kim Joo-in trained at Washington University in St. Louis, and you can walk in without an appointment.

FAQ

  • No appointment needed. Walk in during clinic hours: weekdays 09:15–18:15, Saturday 09:15–14:00 (lunch break 12:50–13:50). Closed on Sundays and holidays.

  • Tourists do not need a passport to see the doctor. Please bring it if you can: your medical record and any prescription need an ID number, and for tourists this is usually the passport number. If you live in Korea and use Korean health insurance (NHIS), bring your ARC or passport. By law, the clinic must check a photo ID before it applies your insurance.

  • You can pay by credit card or cash. Foreign Visa and Mastercard cards are accepted.
