    Adult Vaccines for Foreign Residents in Busan: Hepatitis A and B, Tdap, Shingles, Pneumonia

    Living in Busan and need a vaccine? Kim Joo-in Internal Medicine, 5 minutes from Seomyeon Station, gives the main adult vaccines. The doctor checks which ones you need, in English.

    ☎ Call 051-802-7550 Walk-ins welcome · 5 min from Seomyeon Station Exit 7

    In short

    Five adult vaccines you can get in Seomyeon

    Kim Joo-in Internal Medicine gives vaccines for hepatitis A, hepatitis B, tetanus–diphtheria–whooping cough (Tdap), shingles and pneumococcal pneumonia. You can walk in without an appointment. The doctor looks at your age, health and vaccine record, and tells you which ones you need.

    The vaccines

    What each vaccine is for

    The doses and spacing below are the clinic's schedules.

    2 doses · 0 and 6 months

    Hepatitis A

    A liver infection spread through food and water. The US CDC says any adult who wants protection can get it.

    3 doses · 0, 1 and 6 months

    Hepatitis B

    A liver infection spread through blood and body fluids. The CDC recommends it for adults aged 19 to 59, and for older adults who want it.

    1 dose

    Tdap (tetanus, diphtheria, whooping cough)

    One dose for adults who never had it, then a booster every 10 years. One dose in each pregnancy, in weeks 27 to 36.

    2 doses · 0 and 3 months

    Shingles

    A recombinant shingles vaccine for adults 50 and older, and for adults with a weak immune system.

    1 dose

    Pneumococcal (pneumonia)

    A pneumococcal conjugate vaccine. The CDC recommends it for adults 50 and older, and for younger adults with some long-term conditions.

    Before the shot

    Tell the doctor first if any of these apply

    Fever or feeling unwell today
    The doctor may move the shot to another day.
    A strong allergic reaction to a vaccine before
    The doctor decides if the vaccine is safe for you.
    Pregnancy, or planning a pregnancy
    Tdap is recommended in each pregnancy. For other vaccines, the doctor checks first.
    A weak immune system, or medicine that lowers it
    These vaccines are not live vaccines, but the doctor checks the best timing.

    Not feeling well? Just walk in.

    No appointment needed during clinic hours. Questions? Call us first.

    How it works

    Getting a vaccine, step by step

    1

    Walk in

    No appointment needed. Vaccines can run out, so call 051-802-7550 if you want to be sure.

    2

    See the doctor

    In English. Bring your vaccine record if you have one, even from another country.

    3

    Get the shot and wait

    Stay in the clinic for about 15 to 30 minutes after the shot, so the staff can watch for a reaction.

    4

    Pay and plan the next dose

    Pay by credit card or cash. Foreign Visa and Mastercard cards are accepted. The front desk tells you when to come back for the next dose.

    Price and records

    Price and proof of vaccination

    Prices depend on the vaccine. Please ask the front desk or call 051-802-7550.

    If your employer or school needs proof of vaccination, ask at the front desk. English certificates are available.

    Frequently asked questions

    Adult vaccines in Busan: common questions

    Do I need an appointment for a vaccine?

    No. You can walk in during clinic hours. Vaccines can run out, so call 051-802-7550 if you want to be sure.

    How much do the vaccines cost?

    Prices depend on the vaccine. Please ask the front desk or call 051-802-7550.

    I started a vaccine series in another country. Can I finish it here?

    Usually yes. Bring your vaccine record, so the doctor can see which dose you need next.

    Can I get Tdap during pregnancy?

    The US CDC recommends one dose of Tdap in each pregnancy, in weeks 27 to 36. Tell the doctor that you are pregnant, and check with your obstetrician too.

    How long do I stay after the shot?

    About 15 to 30 minutes, so the staff can watch for a reaction.

    How do I pay?

    By credit card or cash. Foreign Visa and Mastercard cards are accepted.
