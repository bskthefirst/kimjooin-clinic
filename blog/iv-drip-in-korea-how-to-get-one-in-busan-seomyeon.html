    IV Drips in Korea (수액): What They Are and How to Get One in Busan

    In Korea, many people get a nutrient IV drip (수액, “suaek”) at a local clinic when they feel run down or are getting over a cold. Here is what is in it, how long it takes, and how to get one in Seomyeon.

    ☎ Call 051-802-7550 Walk-ins welcome · 5 min from Seomyeon Station Exit 7

    In short

    The doctor checks you first. Then 30 minutes to 1.5 hours

    At Kim Joo-in Internal Medicine, the doctor first asks how you feel and checks your blood pressure and health history. Then you lie down in the IV room while the drip runs. A licorice and vitamin shot takes about 30–40 minutes. An amino-acid nutrient drip takes about 1 hour, and the fatigue-recovery set takes up to 1.5 hours.

    Why IV drips

    Why do Koreans get IV drips?

    Neighborhood clinics in Korea commonly offer nutrient IV drips. People get one when they are tired, after a cold or the flu, or when they cannot eat or drink well.

    A drip puts fluids, amino acids and vitamins straight into a vein. This makes it an easy way to top up when eating is hard.

    A richer amino-acid nutrient drip with added vitamins.

    Safety

    Tell the doctor first if any of these apply

    High blood pressure
    Licorice can raise blood pressure and lower potassium in some people.
    Heart or kidney disease
    Extra fluid can be a strain, so the doctor decides if a drip is safe for you.
    Pregnancy or allergies to medicines
    Tell the doctor before any injection.
    Fever, cough or sore throat
    The doctor first checks the cause, for example with a rapid flu or COVID test.

    Frequently asked questions

    IV drips in Busan: common questions

    Can tourists get an IV drip in Busan?

    Yes. At Kim Joo-in Internal Medicine you can walk in without an appointment. The doctor checks you first, then you rest in the IV room. Tourists do not need a passport, but please bring it if you can.

    How long does an IV drip take?

    About 30–40 minutes for the licorice and vitamin shot, about 1 hour for an amino-acid nutrient drip, and up to 1.5 hours for the fatigue-recovery set.

    How much does an IV drip cost?

    The price depends on the drip. Please ask at the front desk or call 051-802-7550.

    Can I get an IV drip when I have a cold?

    The doctor first checks the cause of your symptoms. If you have a fever, a rapid flu or COVID test may come first. If you cannot eat or drink well, the doctor may suggest a drip.

    Can everyone get an IV drip?

    The doctor checks first. Tell the doctor if you have high blood pressure, heart or kidney disease, are pregnant, or have allergies to medicines.

    How do I pay?

    By credit card or cash. Foreign Visa and Mastercard cards are accepted.
